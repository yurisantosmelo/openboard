## Overview

Every Organization starts with its Default Workspace, and Organization Owners can create more. Whoever acts as Workspace Owner can rename a Workspace, change its visibility, archive/restore it or delete it.

An Organization always keeps at least one active (non-Archived, non-Deleted) Workspace: archiving or deleting its last active Workspace fails with `409 Conflict` (`last-active-workspace`). Archiving a Workspace makes all of its Projects read-only until restored.
