## Overview

OpenBoard Platform ships the backend as a single deployable. [[service|IdentityModule]], [[service|OrganizationsModule]] and [[service|WorkModule]] are modules of that deployable: they keep separate boundaries and call each other in process through injected ports, never importing another module's internals.

| Component | Type | Responsibility |
|-----------|------|----------------|
| [[service\|IdentityModule]] | Backend module | Users, passwords, sessions, deactivation and Temporary Passwords |
| [[service\|OrganizationsModule]] | Backend module | Organizations, Workspaces, Organization and Workspace Memberships and Lifecycle |
