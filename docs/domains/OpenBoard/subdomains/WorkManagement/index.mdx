## Overview

Work Management is the core domain of OpenBoard (ADR 0010). Every [[entity|Project]] belongs to one [[entity|Workspace]] and defines its own [[entity|Status]] list, [[entity|WorkItemType]] hierarchy and optional [[entity|TransitionRule]] constraints while remaining comparable across Projects through the four fixed Status Groups (`not_committed`, `planned`, `started`, `completed`).

Teams organize responsibility inside a Workspace without controlling access (ADR 0005), and can be associated with Projects as [[entity|ProjectTeam]] records. Moving a Project to another Workspace of the same Organization preserves its Work Items and workflow while dropping its Project Teams.
