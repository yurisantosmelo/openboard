## Overview

A Workspace Membership exists only while the same User holds an Organization Membership in that Workspace's Organization; removing the Organization Membership automatically removes every Workspace Membership in that Organization.

Organization Owners and Administrators act as Workspace Owner in every Workspace without needing a Workspace Membership.
