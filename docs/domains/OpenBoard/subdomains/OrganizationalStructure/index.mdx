## Overview

The [[entity|Organization]] is the tenant: its data never crosses into another Organization (ADR 0003). A [[entity|Workspace]] divides one Organization and can restrict visibility inside it. Access is granted through [[entity|OrganizationMembership]] and [[entity|WorkspaceMembership]].

Workspaces can be Archived (hidden from default lists and read-only, making all of their Projects read-only) and restored intact. Both Organizations and Workspaces support Pending Deletion with a configurable grace period before permanent deletion.
